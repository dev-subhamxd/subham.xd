01 — about

A student who ships.

I'm Subham Panda, currently in the science stream at school, but most of my real learning happens after homework: in Discord, in a code editor, in the small hours spent tuning a hover state until it feels right.

Over the past few years I've designed and grown Discord communities from the ground up — structure, roles, channels, moderation flow, the whole feel of a server — and built the bots that run underneath them, whether that's a quick BDFD build or something custom in discord.js or discord.py.

On the web side, I build with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, and when a client needs their site in someone's pocket, I package it into an installable Android app with WebtoNative.

03 — why me

The gap I fill.

Most servers and sites are left half-finished.

A community gets started with good intentions and no real structure, so it goes quiet in a month. A bot gets bolted on from a template and breaks the moment someone asks for something specific. A website gets built once and never touched again, because the person who made it isn't around to maintain it.

None of that is a tools problem. It's a follow-through problem — and it's the part most freelancers skip once the invoice is paid.
